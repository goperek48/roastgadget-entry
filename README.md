Entry for make.fun's **One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day** bounty
(`u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610`).

`main.py` writes one original one-line roast to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My AirPods last four hours — unless I'm on a call, when the left one quits at minute eleven and every meeting ends with me sounding kidnapped.

One line, 142 characters. The joke is the betrayal with a stopwatch:
four hours of battery on their own, eleven minutes the second a call
starts — and the left bud always goes first, so the meeting hears the
kidnapping, not the goodbye.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (validated: a single line, 1–200 characters,
UTF-8).
